1. Scope & controller

This policy covers the Deerby mobile app and any associated services (collectively, the "Service"). The data controller for personal data processed through the Service is Mustafa Mecit, reachable at support@musamecit.com. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you can contact us about any data-protection question at the same address.

2. Age requirement

Deerby is for users 18 years or older. By creating an account, you confirm that you are at least 18. We will permanently delete any account we discover to belong to a minor.

3. Data we collect

3.1 Account data (provided by you or your sign-in provider)

  • Authentication identifier from Apple Sign-In or Google (a stable user ID, plus the email address you choose to share).
  • Display name, date of birth, gender, who you're "looking for", optional bio, optional height, optional relationship status.
  • 3 to 6 profile photos (uploaded by you).
  • Filter preferences (which genders, ages, and "looking for" categories you want to see and to receive messages from).
  • Language preference.

3.2 Location data

  • Your precise GPS coordinates while you use the app, with foreground access only.
  • The timestamp of your most recent location update.
  • We do not track your location in the background or build a movement history beyond your most recent point.

3.3 Communication data

  • Messages you send and receive, plus metadata (sender, conversation, timestamps, read receipts).
  • A flag indicating whether a message used a Premium "long-range" slot (sent outside the 105 m radius).

3.4 Safety & moderation data

  • Blocks you create, reports you submit, and reports submitted about you.

3.5 Technical & device data

  • Device platform (iOS / Android), app version, OS version, locale, time zone.
  • Push notification tokens (when you opt in).
  • Crash and error logs, with stack traces and non-identifying device attributes.

3.6 Purchase data

  • Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing receipts and transaction identifiers (we do not see your card details — payment is handled by Apple or Google).
  • Subscription status and expiry date.

5. Precise location and the 105 m radius

Deerby is built around a strict locality rule. By default, you can only see and be seen by users within 105 metres of your current precise location. Outside that radius you do not appear in their map and they do not appear in yours, regardless of mutual interest. This radius is enforced server-side by the discovery query (discover_nearby) and the messaging permission check (can_message).

Premium users can browse the map outside the 105 m radius and send up to 10 long-range messages per active subscription period.

The app refuses to operate without precise (full-accuracy) location permission. You can turn off location anytime; the app will then become inactive.

6. Ghost mode

You can enable Ghost mode at any time. While Ghost mode is on, your profile is hidden from everyone and you cannot see anyone else. Your location is also not written to our servers while Ghost mode is on.

7. Messages, mutual rule, 24-hour auto-delete

If User A sends the first message of a conversation and User B does not reply within 24 hours, the conversation is permanently deleted from our database, including all of its messages. Once both users have sent at least one message, the conversation becomes mutual and will be retained until either user deletes it or deletes their account. We display a clear warning at the start of every new conversation.

8. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the following categories of recipients, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Supabase, Inc. — our backend and database provider (authentication, Postgres database, file storage, realtime). Data is processed under a Data Processing Addendum.
  • Apple Inc. — Apple Sign-In and StoreKit purchases (iOS).
  • Google LLC — Google Sign-In, Play Billing, and (Android only) Google Maps.
  • Apple Push Notification Service / Firebase Cloud Messaging — for delivering push notifications when you opt in.
  • Law-enforcement or regulators, when we are legally required.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with advertising networks.

9. International data transfers

Our backend may store data on servers located outside your country (typically in the EU or the United States, depending on the Supabase region you connect to). Where required, transfers are governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

10. Security

We use TLS in transit, at-rest encryption on our managed database and object storage, and strict row-level-security policies so that one user can only read or modify their own private data. Authentication tokens are stored in the device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials safe.

11. Retention

  • Profile and photos: as long as your account exists.
  • Location: only the most recent point is kept; it is overwritten on every update and cleared 30 days after you go inactive.
  • Non-mutual conversations: deleted automatically 24 hours after the first message if there is no reply.
  • Mutual conversations: until either party deletes the conversation or their account.
  • IAP receipts: retained for the duration of your subscription plus the period required by tax/audit law (typically 6 years).
  • Crash logs: maximum 90 days.

12. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. You can exercise the most common rights directly in the app:

  • Access & correction: from Settings → Profile.
  • Deletion: from Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your profile, photos, blocks, filters, and chats.
  • For any other request, write to support@musamecit.com.

13. Children

Deerby is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used Deerby, please contact us immediately and we will delete the account.

14. Cookies and analytics

The mobile app does not use browser cookies. We use a minimal set of crash and product analytics events (no advertising IDs). You can disable analytics permission from your device's privacy settings (iOS: "Allow Apps to Track" / "Analytics & Improvements"; Android: opt-out of personalised data sharing).

15. Premium & in-app purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple In-App Purchase on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. We do not see or store payment credentials. Receipt validation is performed server-side. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their store policies. Auto-renewal is managed in your store account.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced inside the app at least 7 days before they take effect. The "Effective date" above always reflects the current version. Past versions are available on request.

17. Contact

Questions, requests, complaints: support@musamecit.com.
