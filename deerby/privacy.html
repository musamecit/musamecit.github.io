3. Categories of personal data we collect

3.1 Identity & account data

  • Authentication identifier from Apple Sign-In and/or Google Sign-In (a stable user ID, plus the email address you choose to share with us).
  • Display name you choose.
  • Date of birth, used to calculate your age and to enforce the 18+ requirement; only the year/month/day is stored.
  • Apple's real-user-status hint, if available (a coarse indicator from Apple of whether your sign-in looks like a real person — three values: unknown, likelyReal, likelyFake).

3.2 Profile data

  • Gender (one of: male / female / non-binary / other).
  • "Looking for" preference (friendship, dating, relationship, networking, open).
  • Optional: relationship status, height, bio (max 500 characters).
  • 3 to 6 profile photos that you upload (you may delete or replace them at any time).
  • Up to 3 icebreaker prompts (a prompt key + a short answer of yours).
  • Vibe / status emoji and an optional 4-hour expiry.
  • App language preference.

3.3 Location data

  • Precise GPS coordinates while you have the app open (foreground only). We never request "Always Allow" background location.
  • The timestamp of your most recent location update (location_updated_at).
  • If you enable Approximate location, we apply a deterministic ±30 m offset to the coordinates we expose to other users — your stored point is fuzzed before any discovery query reads it. Your raw point is still stored on our servers; the fuzz is applied for visibility, not in‑memory before write.
  • When you create a Safe Zone, we store the centre (latitude, longitude) and radius (25–500 m).
  • When you buy a Boost, we store the centre point of where the boost was purchased.

3.4 Communications & engagement

  • Messages you send and receive, including timestamps, read receipts (read_at) and a flag for long-range (Premium‑gated) messages.
  • Encounters log: an anonymised pair record (you, another user, ~250 m grid cell, time) used to detect "familiar face" alerts. The pair is canonicalised (smaller user-ID first) so we don't know who saw whom first.
  • Profile visits (when you open someone's profile we record a row; both sides must have "show me as a visitor" on for either side to ever see the row).
  • Blocks, mutes, reports you create or receive.
  • Gifts you send/receive (gift type, conversation, timestamps; no message content).

3.5 Verification & safety

  • Verification selfies you submit (stored in a private storage bucket; never shown to other users; deleted when verification is approved or rejected, and within 30 days otherwise).
  • If you opt in to optional ID verification (V1.4+ feature), the document data is processed by our verification provider (Stripe Identity); we receive only the result and a session reference.
  • Photo content moderation labels returned by AWS Rekognition (e.g., "Suggestive", "Violence"); these labels stay on the photo row even after the photo is deleted, for fraud-detection trail.

3.6 Device, technical & diagnostic data

  • Device platform (iOS or Android), OS version, app version, locale, time zone.
  • Push-notification token (Apple APNs / Google FCM), only when you grant push permission.
  • Crash logs (anonymised stack traces, no message bodies, no email).
  • Coarse usage events for app stability (e.g., feature timings) — never linked to ad networks.

3.7 Purchase data

  • Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing receipts, transaction identifiers, the product purchased, expiry dates and subscription status. We never see your card or bank details — payment is fully handled by Apple or Google.

3.8 Consent log

  • Every time you accept the Terms, the Privacy Policy or the 18+ attestation, we write a row to consent_log with: your user ID, what you accepted, the document version, your platform/OS string, and the timestamp. This is required to defend against fraudulent disputes and to comply with regulator audits.

6. Precise location, the 105 m rule, and ±30 m fuzz

Deerby's discovery rule is enforced server-side. Specifically, the SQL function discover_nearby filters candidates with PostGIS ST_DWithin(location, target, 105). Premium users can ask the same function with a larger radius. Your coordinates only leave our servers in three places:

  1. Inside discover_nearby when the caller is you (we don't return your own row).
  2. As anonymised aggregated bins inside discover_heatmap (cells with fewer than 3 users are hidden — k-anonymity).
  3. To you, when you display your own pin on your own map.

If you enable Approximate location, the discovery and heatmap queries apply a deterministic ±30 m offset before any distance comparison. This means strangers can never tell whether you are at coordinates X or coordinates Y±30 m. Your stored point is still your real point — we do this so that turning the toggle off restores accuracy without losing any data. The ±30 m offset is keyed to a stable random seed per user so it doesn't fluctuate over time.

We never operate your location in the background. Apple's iOS will always show the blue location indicator when our app is using your position. The Android equivalent is the green / blue shield icon.

7. Ghost mode & safe zones

While Ghost mode is on:

  • Other users do not see you in discover_nearby.
  • You do not see other users either (this is a mutual rule by design).
  • Your client stops writing your location to the server. Your last known point remains until it is overwritten or cleared by the offline-cron after 10 minutes.

Safe zones (your home, work, gym, etc.) auto-enable Ghost mode the moment your phone reports a position inside the zone radius. We never log "User entered Safe Zone X" events; we just flip the bit on the profile row.

Quiet hours work similarly: during the window you set, a SQL cron flips Ghost on, and the discovery query treats you as if you were already in Ghost.

8. Messages, the 24-hour rule & encryption in transit

Messages are stored in our database in plaintext; they are not end-to-end encrypted. They are protected:

  • In transit, by TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS to Supabase, encrypted realtime websockets).
  • At rest, by managed disk encryption on the Supabase Postgres cluster.
  • By row-level security (RLS) policies, so only the two participants can read a row.

If User A sends the first message of a new conversation and User B does not reply within 24 hours, the conversation row and every message inside it are permanently deleted from our active database by an automated job (every 5 minutes). Once both sides have replied at least once, the conversation becomes mutual and is retained until either party deletes it or deletes their account. Deleted messages cannot be restored.

13. Security measures

We use, among others, the following safeguards:

  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies on every table — database-level enforcement that one user can only read or modify rows that belong to them.
  • TLS 1.2+ in transit; certificate pinning is on our roadmap.
  • Disk encryption at rest on the managed Postgres cluster and object storage.
  • Authentication tokens kept in iOS Keychain / Android Keystore.
  • Sign in with Apple uses a cryptographic 256-bit nonce per attempt.
  • Edge Functions run with the service-role JWT only on the server side and never expose it to the client.
  • Private bucket for selfies (never publicly readable, never directly URL-accessible).
  • Automated photo content moderation (AWS Rekognition) with a 30-minute fallback so failed checks default to a permissive but auditable state.
  • Rate limiting at the platform level; abuse protections at the application level.

No system is perfectly secure. We rely on you to keep your sign-in credentials safe.

14. Your rights

Under GDPR (Articles 15–22), the UK GDPR, KVKK (m.11), CCPA/CPRA, the LGPD (Brazil) and equivalent laws, you have the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object, port your data and not be subject to automated decision-making with legal effect. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

How to exercise the most common rights, directly in the app:

  • Access & portability: Settings → Download my data exports a machine-readable JSON of every row attached to your account.
  • Rectification: Settings → Profile / Edit profile / Filters / Safety & Privacy.
  • Erasure: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, photos (in both buckets), conversations, blocks, filters, push tokens, gifts, encounters and your auth identity. Receipts and the consent log are retained as required by law for the period in section 12.
  • Restriction / objection: write to privacy@musamecit.com.
  • No automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effect is performed by Deerby. Profile visibility ranking, content moderation flags and verification scores are advisory; a human can always override them.

We respond to verifiable requests within 30 days (extendable by 60 days for complex requests, with notice).

Deerby is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a minor is using Deerby, please contact us at safety@musamecit.com and we will delete the account on the same business day.

16. Cookies, analytics, "Do Not Track"

The mobile app does not use browser cookies. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs and we do not collect IDFA or Google Advertising ID. We do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" headers because the mobile app does not use a browser. We honour iOS App Tracking Transparency by simply not asking — we do not track users across apps or websites.

17. Premium & in-app purchases

All purchases (subscriptions, gifts, boosts) are processed by Apple In-App Purchase on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. We do not see your payment instrument. Receipt validation is performed server-side by our validate-iap Edge Function, which calls Apple's verifyReceipt endpoint or the Play Developer API. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their store policies. Subscription auto-renewal is managed entirely in your store account.

18. Deceased users

If you let us know that an account belongs to someone who has died and you can prove your relationship to them and/or your legal authority, we can either delete the account or, where law permits, freeze it for a successor's claim. Write to privacy@musamecit.com.

Türkiye notifications under KVKK m.12/5 will be filed with the Kurul "in the shortest time" (typically interpreted as 72 hours).
