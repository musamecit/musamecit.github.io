3. How Deerby works

Deerby shows you other users within 105 metres of your current precise location, filtered by the preferences you set (gender, age range, looking-for). You may tap a user's profile to view it and may message them subject to the filter and proximity rules described below. Search by name is intentionally not available — discovery is location-based only. Deerby is not a matchmaking, escort, dating-arrangement, mail-order-bride or compensated-companionship service, and may not be used as such.

Deerby cannot function without precise (full-accuracy) location permission. By using the Service you authorise Deerby to read your foreground location and send it to our backend solely to (i) determine other users within 105 m of you and (ii) make you discoverable to those users (subject to your filter, ghost, quiet-hours and safe-zone settings). If you revoke location permission or your device reports a low-accuracy location, the Service will pause until permission is restored. We do not request "Always Allow" background location.

5. Ghost mode & safe zones

You can enable Ghost mode at any time. While Ghost mode is on, your profile is hidden from every other user and you cannot view other users either. Your location is not written to our servers during Ghost. Ghost mode does not pause your subscription; Premium time continues to elapse normally. Safe zones (your home, work, gym, etc.) auto-enable Ghost the moment your phone reports a position inside the zone radius. Quiet hours auto-enable Ghost during the time window you set. None of these features are infallible; you remain responsible for your own appearance/non-appearance on the map.

6. Conversations & the 24-hour rule

If you start a conversation by sending the first message and the other user does not reply within 24 hours, the conversation and all of its messages are permanently deleted from our servers by an automated job. Once each side has sent at least one message, the conversation becomes mutual and is retained until either party deletes it or deletes their account. We display a clear visual countdown on every non-mutual conversation. We do not guarantee delivery, recovery, or restoration of deleted conversations. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; see our Privacy Policy section 8.

Setting a filter does not guarantee specific results — discovery depends on who is online and within range. Boosts give a temporary visibility lift; they do not bypass the 18+ requirement, blocks, or filters set by other users.
